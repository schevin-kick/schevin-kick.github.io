Blocklike Support

Help for the Blocklike block puzzle game for iPhone.

Contact
Report a bug or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/schevin-kick/schevin-kick.github.io/issues. If the app hit an error, open Settings in the app and tap Send error report, then paste the report into your message.

How to play

Drag the three pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 board. Fill a full row or column to clear it. Clearing several lines at once, or on back-to-back moves (a combo), scores more. The run ends when none of your pieces fit.

Common questions

What are perks?
In an endless run you pick one of three perks at the start and at every new stage. Perks last for the whole run and some can stack.
What are the gray stones?
From stage 6, if you go several moves without clearing a line, stones drop onto empty cells. They clear like normal blocks when you complete a line through them.
What is the Daily Board?
The same 20 rounds of pieces for everyone each day, with no perks. Beat the bot's score for gold. You get one attempt per day, plus bonus attempts by watching an ad.
How do revives work?
When you're stuck in an endless run you can watch an ad to clear space and keep going, up to three times per run.
How do I change my ad privacy choices?
On iOS, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EU, UK, Switzerland and some US states, open the app's Settings and tap Privacy choices.
Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Progress is stored only on your device, and there are no accounts.
