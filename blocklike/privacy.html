Blocklike Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Blocklike is a block puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress (best scores, Daily Board results, settings such as sound and vibration) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. Blocklike has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, or other contact details.

If the app hits an error, a short error report (the error message and game state such as stage and score) is saved on your device. It is only sent anywhere if you choose to share it from Settings.

Advertising

Blocklike is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, operating system, language) and how you interact with ads.

On iOS, you choose whether apps may use your advertising identifier through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and you can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you do not allow tracking, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized using that identifier.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can review or change your choice at any time from the app's Settings screen ("Privacy choices").

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for advertising at any time from the app's Settings screen ("Privacy choices").

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

Blocklike is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
